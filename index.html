Chess

Two players

One board, two people, one device. The board turns to face whoever is to move, so nobody plays upside down.

or

Create a room and send the link. The board lives on the server, so neither side can move out of turn or invent a move the rules do not allow.

Arcade

Moves

Full rules: castling, en passant, promotion, checkmate and stalemate, plus the draws that are easy to forget, threefold repetition, the fifty move rule and dead positions with too little material to mate. Pass and play runs entirely in this page and sends nothing anywhere. Against a friend the match is refereed by a server that owns the board: it decides whose turn it is and which moves are legal, so a tampered page can ask to move a pinned knight or castle out of check and simply be told no. There is no leaderboard, because a match needs two people and any ranking two people can agree on in private is a ranking they can farm.
